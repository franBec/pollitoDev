Hi! I'm Franco Becvort — I go by **Pollito** online. I'm a software developer living in Lisbon, Portugal, originally from Argentina.

![Franco Becvort](/about/profile.png)

I build software across the stack, from LLM-powered developer tools to cloud-native microservices handling hundreds of thousands of requests a day. I care about clean architecture, reproducible systems, and tools that make other developers more productive.

Right now I'm a core contributor to **Velx**, an AI platform that analyzes, distills, and documents large-scale codebases (named in Gartner's first Magic Quadrant for its category). My day-to-day involves MCP server tooling, distillation engines, React-based diagram editors, and LLM evaluation benchmarks — all in TypeScript, Deno, and Python.

Before the AI work, I spent years in the Java and Spring Boot ecosystem, focused on authentication systems, identity migrations, and multi-cloud deployments for fintech and enterprise clients.

When I'm not at the keyboard, you'll find me tinkering with my NixOS setup, reading, or exploring Lisbon.
